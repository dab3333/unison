Logo

Two overlapping circles are two people. Where they overlap, the play button appears: everyone in unison. Violet and coral blend into an amber core.

Voice and rules

  • Warm, short, a little playful: "Press play, together."
  • Lowercase wordmark. Sentence case everywhere else.
  • Flat color only: no gradients, glows, or glass effects. Violet is the single action color; amber marks emphasis.
  • Icons are simple solid SVGs. No emoji in the interface.
  • Every tap target is at least 44px. Phones are the design baseline.
  • Contrast: Text on Night is above 15:1. Dark text sits on the bright brand colors.
